Describes something that **exists only briefly and then disappears**.

In tech it usually means "temporary, disposable": an ephemeral container (thrown away after use), an ephemeral port (a temporary network port), ephemeral storage (wiped on restart).

> The container's filesystem is **ephemeral** — anything not written to a volume is lost on restart.
